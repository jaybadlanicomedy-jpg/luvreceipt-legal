Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026. This policy describes how LuvReceipt handles information when you use our mobile app.

Who we are

LuvReceipt is operated by Jay Badlani Entertainment LLC (jaybadlanientertainmentllc@gmail.com) for personal entertainment and relationship journaling only. The app helps couples and individuals log affectionate spending and memories in a playful, consensual way—not professional accounting or financial advice.

Local data on your device

LuvReceipt is local-first: partner profiles, photos, memory notes, and logged entries stay stored locally on your device unless you export them manually or sync via Couple Sync. We do not upload your full ledger by default.

AI receipt scanning

When you choose to scan a receipt, receipt images are transmitted securely over HTTPS to Google Gemini solely to extract merchant, date, category, and amount text. Images are not stored on our servers, are not sold, and are not retained by LuvReceipt after processing.

Live Couple Sync

If you opt in, Live Couple Sync stores only anonymous random 8-character couple room codes (shown as LUV-XXXXXXXX), the display names you choose, and basic entry titles, amounts, categories, and dates needed to compare spending with a partner who has your code. We never store or sync photos, memory notes, passwords, or bank credentials. You can permanently delete your cloud room at any time using “Unlink & Delete Cloud Sync Data” in partner settings.

Usage analytics

To improve app stability, LuvReceipt collects anonymous, aggregated app usage metrics (such as app opens and feature counts) via PostHog, tied to a random device ID created on your phone—not your name, email, or app-store account. Session Replay is disabled. We do not track personal names, receipt contents, or photos.

Age Requirement & US State Privacy Rights

LuvReceipt is intended for adults and users aged 17 and older and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Jay Badlani Entertainment LLC does NOT sell or share personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising. Users in California and other US states may delete synced cloud data anytime using the in-app “Unlink & Delete Cloud Sync Data” button or by contacting jaybadlanientertainmentllc@gmail.com.

International & EEA/UK Privacy Rights

Minimal anonymous sync and usage metrics are processed based on your consent when you enable AI receipt scanning or Live Couple Sync, and on our legitimate interest in operating the app. For any privacy inquiries, contact jaybadlanientertainmentllc@gmail.com.

Backups

Export creates a file on your device that you control. Import replaces local data on that device only. Backup files may contain photos and notes—keep them somewhere safe if you share or store them.

Data Security

If a security incident ever impacts synced cloud room metadata, we will investigate promptly and provide notice as required by applicable law.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to jaybadlanientertainmentllc@gmail.com or to the contact address listed with Jay Badlani Entertainment LLC on the App Store or Google Play listing.
